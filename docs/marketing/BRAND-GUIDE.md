# customerSAX — Brand System v0.2

## Brand idea
**Customer service first. AI-native underneath.**

customerSAX is the shared operating layer for human agents, AI agents, supervisors and the systems required to resolve customer needs.

## Logo meaning
The icon is intentionally operational rather than “AI magical.” Two service paths enter from the left, converge on a shared customer/context node, and leave as one resolved path. This can represent:
- human + AI workers,
- conversation + transaction context,
- channel + system-of-record,
- service request + business policy.

The wordmark keeps **customer** lowercase to feel human and accessible. **SAX** is uppercase/cobalt to identify the platform/system layer.

## Typography
Use a modern grotesk / UI sans. Preferred: **Inter**. Fallback: system sans (`-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, Arial).

## Voice
- Explain customer-service value before AI mechanics.
- Say **human agents and AI agents** when discussing workforce.
- Use **resolution** instead of deflection as the primary outcome.
- Avoid “AI magic,” “replace agents,” and vague autonomy claims.
- Describe risk, policy and approvals explicitly.
